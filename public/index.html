What Is P&L in Trading?

P&L stands for Profit and Loss. It represents the financial result of a trading position — the difference between the price at which you enter a trade and the price at which you exit, multiplied by the number of units traded. A positive P&L means the trade is profitable; a negative P&L means a loss.

How to Calculate Profit and Loss

The basic formula is: P&L = (Exit Price − Entry Price) × Quantity. This calculator determines the asset quantity from your invested capital and entry price, then computes both the potential profit at your take profit level and the potential loss at your stop loss level.

How This Calculator Works

Enter four values: invested capital, entry price, take profit price, and stop loss price. The calculator shows the quantity of the asset you would acquire, the net profit if the take profit is reached, and the net loss if the stop loss is hit — all expressed in both absolute terms and as a percentage of your capital.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does P&L mean in trading?

P&L stands for Profit and Loss. It measures how much money a trade has made or lost.

How do you calculate profit on a trade?

Profit equals (Take Profit Price minus Entry Price) times Quantity. The quantity is your invested capital divided by the entry price.

How do you calculate loss on a trade?

Loss equals (Stop Loss Price minus Entry Price) times Quantity. This shows the maximum potential loss for the position.

What is the difference between take profit and stop loss?

Take profit is the target price to close a winning trade. Stop loss is the safety limit to close a losing trade and prevent further losses.

Does this calculator include commissions?

No. This calculator does not factor in broker commissions, spreads, or swap fees.

Can I use this for any type of trade?

Yes. The calculator works for any market — forex, stocks, crypto, or others — as long as you know the entry price, take profit, and stop loss values.
